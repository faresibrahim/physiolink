PhysioLink Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

This policy explains what information the PhysioLink app (“the app”) handles, why, how it is protected, and the choices you have. The app is owned and operated by MoveLink Ltd. (“MoveLink”, “we”, “us”).

1. Who is responsible

MoveLink Ltd. is responsible for the personal data processed through the app.

Privacy and data requests: physiolinkco@gmail.com
WhatsApp: +972 59 233 3972

2. How PhysioLink works

PhysioLink is used by patients of physiotherapy clinics that work with us. You do not sign up in the app: your clinic creates your account and gives you your login details. Your clinic’s therapists assign your exercises and manage your appointments; the app shows them to you, lets you record how each exercise felt, and lets you request appointments.

3. Information we handle

Account and contact information (entered by your clinic): first and last name, username, phone number, email address (optional), your clinic and your assigned therapist.

Health information:

  • the diagnosis or condition your clinic records for you;
  • your exercise program (exercises, sets, repetitions, frequency) and its progress;
  • the effort or difficulty rating you submit after an exercise, and when you completed it;
  • your appointments and appointment requests, including their date, time, status and any notes;
  • documents your clinic attaches to your record, such as reports.

Device and app information: a push-notification token for your device, your daily reminder setting (on/off and time), and your language preference (stored only on your device). Your sign-in session is stored in your device’s secure storage (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore on Android).

Technical information: when the app talks to our servers, standard technical data such as your IP address and request time is processed by our hosting provider to deliver the service and keep it secure.

What we do not collect: we do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, payment information, or advertising identifiers. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

4. Why we use it

  • to sign you in and keep your account secure;
  • to show you the exercise program and appointments your clinic sets up, and to let you request and manage appointments;
  • to share your exercise ratings and progress with your clinic so your therapist can follow your treatment;
  • to send notifications you have allowed, such as appointment updates and exercise reminders;
  • to operate, maintain, secure and troubleshoot the service.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or marketing.

5. Who can see it

  • Your clinic: authorised staff and therapists at your clinic can see and manage your information. Each clinic can only see its own patients.
  • Service providers who process data on our behalf, only as needed to run the app:
    • Railway — hosting of our servers and database;
    • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service — delivering notifications;
    • Google Fonts — delivering the fonts the app displays;
    • YouTube — playing exercise videos that are hosted there.
  • MoveLink Ltd.: as the operator of PhysioLink, a small number of authorised MoveLink staff have technical access to the database. They use it only to run, support, secure and fix the service, or to respond to a legal request as described below, and they are bound by confidentiality. We never sell your information or use it for advertising.
  • Authorities and legal requests: we may disclose information when we are required to by law, by a court order, or by a valid request from a competent government or law-enforcement authority; when it is necessary to protect someone’s life or safety; or to establish or defend legal claims.

These providers may process data on servers outside your country. We use them only for the purposes above.

When we receive a legal request, we check that it is lawful and disclose only the information it requires. Where the law allows, we inform your clinic and you before disclosing.

6. How we protect it

  • all traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS;
  • passwords are never stored in readable form, only as secure one-way hashes;
  • access to records is limited to your own account in the app, and to your own clinic in the clinic system;
  • your sign-in session is kept in your device’s secure storage.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and to fix any issue we find promptly.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active with your clinic. When your account is deleted at your request, we permanently erase your personal and health information from our live database within 30 days. Copies in backups are removed when those backups expire.

The only exception is information the law requires us to keep for longer, or information needed for a legal request, investigation or dispute that is under way. In that case we keep only that information, only for as long as required, and then delete it.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction: you can see your profile and program in the app, and update some profile details there. To correct anything else, contact your clinic or us.
  • Account and data deletion: you can ask us to delete your account and all associated data by emailing physiolinkco@gmail.com or messaging us on WhatsApp at +972 59 233 3972. Please include your username and the phone number on your account so we can verify that the request is yours. We will confirm when the deletion is complete. You can also ask your clinic to request it for you. See our support page for details.
  • Notifications: you can turn off notifications at any time in your phone’s settings, and turn the daily reminder on or off in the app.

9. Children

The app is not intended to be used on its own by children under 13. A clinic may create an account for a younger patient only with the consent of the patient’s parent or guardian, who may make any of the requests above on the child’s behalf.

10. Medical information

PhysioLink delivers exercise programs and appointment information set by your clinic’s therapists. It does not diagnose conditions or replace professional medical advice. If you feel pain or your condition changes, stop the exercise and contact your therapist. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above, and for significant changes we will notify you in the app or through your clinic.

المعلومات الصحية:

  • تقييم الجهد أو الصعوبة الذي ترسله بعد التمرين، ووقت إكماله؛
  • مواعيدك وطلبات المواعيد، بما فيها التاريخ والوقت والحالة وأي ملاحظات؛
  • المستندات التي ترفقها عيادتك بملفك، مثل التقارير.

معلومات الجهاز والتطبيق: رمز الإشعارات الخاص بجهازك، وإعداد التذكير اليومي (تشغيل/إيقاف والوقت)، واللغة المفضّلة (تُحفظ على جهازك فقط). تُحفظ جلسة تسجيل الدخول في التخزين الآمن لجهازك (Keychain على الآيفون وKeystore على أندرويد).

المعلومات التقنية: عند تواصل التطبيق مع خوادمنا، يعالج مزوّد الاستضافة بيانات تقنية معتادة مثل عنوان IP ووقت الطلب، وذلك لتقديم الخدمة والحفاظ على أمانها.

ما لا نجمعه: لا نجمع موقعك، أو جهات الاتصال، أو الصور، أو بيانات الكاميرا أو الميكروفون، أو معلومات الدفع، أو معرّفات الإعلانات. لا يحتوي التطبيق على إعلانات ولا على أدوات تحليل أو تتبّع من أطراف ثالثة.

4. لماذا نستخدمها

  • لتسجيل دخولك والحفاظ على أمان حسابك؛
  • لعرض برنامج التمارين والمواعيد التي تحدّدها عيادتك، وتمكينك من طلب المواعيد وإدارتها؛
  • لمشاركة تقييماتك وتقدّمك مع عيادتك حتى يتابع المعالج علاجك؛
  • لإرسال الإشعارات التي سمحت بها، مثل تحديثات المواعيد وتذكيرات التمارين؛
  • لتشغيل الخدمة وصيانتها وتأمينها وحل مشاكلها.

لا نبيع بياناتك الشخصية، ولا نستخدمها للإعلانات أو التسويق.
