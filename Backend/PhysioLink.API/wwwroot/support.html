Delete your account and data

You can ask us to permanently delete your PhysioLink account and all data linked to it: your profile, diagnosis, exercise program and ratings, appointments and attached documents.

  1. Send an email to physiolinkco@gmail.com with the subject “Account deletion request”, or message us on WhatsApp.
  2. Include your username and the phone number registered on your account, so we can confirm the request is yours.
  3. We delete the account and its data within 30 days and confirm to you when it is done. Copies in backups are removed when those backups expire. The only exception is information the law requires us to keep (see the Privacy Policy).

After deletion you can no longer sign in, and the data cannot be recovered. Your clinic can also submit the request on your behalf.

Common questions

How do I get an account?

Accounts are created by your physiotherapy clinic. Ask your clinic for your username and temporary password.

I forgot my password

Contact your clinic to reset it, or contact us using the details above.

My exercises or appointments look wrong

Your program and appointments are managed by your clinic’s therapist. Contact your clinic to change them.

I am not receiving notifications

Check that notifications are allowed for PhysioLink in your phone’s settings, and that the daily reminder is turned on in the app.

Health notice

PhysioLink shows the exercise program and appointments set by your clinic’s therapists. It does not diagnose conditions or replace professional medical advice. If you feel pain or your condition changes, stop the exercise and contact your therapist. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

حذف حسابك وبياناتك

يمكنك أن تطلب منا حذف حسابك في PhysioLink وجميع البيانات المرتبطة به نهائيًا: ملفك الشخصي، والتشخيص، وبرنامج التمارين وتقييماتك، والمواعيد، والمستندات المرفقة.

  1. أرسل بريدًا إلكترونيًا إلى physiolinkco@gmail.com بعنوان ”طلب حذف حساب“، أو راسلنا عبر واتساب.
  2. اذكر اسم المستخدم ورقم الهاتف المسجّل في حسابك لنتأكد من أن الطلب صادر عنك.
  3. نحذف الحساب وبياناته خلال 30 يومًا ونؤكد لك عند الانتهاء. تُزال النسخ الموجودة في النسخ الاحتياطية عند انتهاء صلاحيتها. الاستثناء الوحيد هو المعلومات التي يُلزمنا القانون بالاحتفاظ بها (راجع سياسة الخصوصية).

بعد الحذف لن تتمكن من تسجيل الدخول، ولا يمكن استرجاع البيانات. يمكن لعيادتك أيضًا تقديم الطلب نيابةً عنك.
